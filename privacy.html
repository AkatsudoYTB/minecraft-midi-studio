Privacy policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026.

Minecraft MIDI Studio is a program that runs on your computer. Its author collects no statistics, shows no advertising and does no tracking. The only information the author receives is about the licenses of the full edition (see License register below).

Your files

Your MIDI files, projects and Minecraft worlds stay on your computer. The program reads them and writes only where you confirm it (installation in a world, with a backup first). Nothing is sent anywhere.

This website

The website itself collects nothing. Its videos are embedded from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com): YouTube is contacted only when you play one.

Official Minecraft sounds

When no Minecraft installation on your computer has the files needed, the program downloads the official sounds and textures from Mojang's servers (piston-meta.mojang.com, resources.download.minecraft.net), as a launcher does. Only these download requests are made; like for any download, Mojang receives your IP address (see the Microsoft privacy statement).

Google sign-in (full edition)

Only when you click Activate with Google or Get my license back with Google, you sign in on Google's own page, in your browser. The program never sees your password. It receives:

  • your e-mail address, to check that the license key was bought for this account;
  • access to the hidden “application data” folder of your Google Drive reserved for this program (scope drive.appdata), where it stores a single file: your license key. It cannot see your other files.

This access is used at once and not kept. Your e-mail address is stored on your computer, in the encrypted activation file, and sent to the author's license register (below). Minecraft MIDI Studio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

License register (full edition)

When the full edition is activated, restored on another computer, and then about once a week while the program is used with internet access, the program sends to the author's license register (a Google Apps Script and a Google Sheets spreadsheet belonging to the author):

  • the license number and the name written in the license;
  • the Google address used to activate it;
  • the version of the program;
  • an anonymous identifier of the computer (a hash: the computer cannot be recognised from it; it only counts the computers of a license).

The register answers whether the license is still valid. It is used only to manage the licenses: help a buyer, send a key again, disable a refunded key or a key published on the internet. Nothing else is sent, and it is never shared or sold. The rows of a license are kept as long as the license exists; ask the author (contact below) to have them deleted. Without internet the full edition keeps working.

Removing your data

  • On your computer: License → Remove the license from this computer, and delete the folders listed in the installation guide.
  • In the author's license register: write to the contact below.
  • In Google Drive: Settings → Manage apps → Minecraft MIDI Studio → Options → Delete hidden app data. You can also remove the program's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Purchases

Payments are handled by the shop (Ko-fi and its payment providers) under their own privacy policies. The author keeps the name, e-mail address and Google address of a buyer only to issue the license key, send it again if needed and manage the license (see above).
