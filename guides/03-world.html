Step 3 — Choose a world (wizard step 1)

Goal: pick the Minecraft world where the music structure will be placed.

  1. On the Home page, click New project. The Wizard opens on step 1, World.
  2. The list shows every world found (official launcher, Modrinth, CurseForge, Prism). A world you create while the program is open appears by itself; Refresh the list forces a new search.
  3. Click a world: its version, game mode, commands and folder are shown below.
  4. Click Use this world.

Good to know

  • Commands must be allowed in the world (the music is controlled with /function). If they are not: in game, Open to LAN → Allow commands, or use a server.
  • Close the world in Minecraft before installing the music (step 10). The software checks it.
  • Create a ZIP backup… copies the whole world into your backup folder without changing it. A backup is also offered automatically before any installation.
  • Nothing is written into the world at this step.
