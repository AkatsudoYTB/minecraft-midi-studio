Step 13 — Playing on a server

Goal: use the music on a multiplayer server (full edition).

  1. In step 11, click Export for a server… and choose a folder. You get:
    • music_mmstudio.zip (the datapack);
    • the resource pack (.zip);
    • README-SERVER.txt: the instructions, with the pack's SHA-1.
  2. Datapack: copy music_mmstudio.zip into <server world>/datapacks/, then type /reload (or restart the server).
  3. Resource pack: put the resource pack at a direct download address (HTTP or HTTPS), then in server.properties:
    resource-pack=<address of the file>
resource-pack-sha1=<SHA-1 given in the instructions>
require-resource-pack=true   (optional)
  1. For the button console: enable-command-block=true in server.properties.
  2. In game, as an operator: /function music:build, then /function music:play.

Players who are not operators control the music with /trigger music.play, /trigger music.volume set 150, etc.

The sounds of the pack are Minecraft's (they belong to Mojang): use them for your server, but do not publish the pack.
