Step 11 — In Minecraft

Goal: choose the place, build the structure and play the music.

Each song has its own commands, from the pack name: “Miss You” becomes music:miss_you, “DDLC” becomes music:ddlc. In this guide, replace <name> with the one of your song (shown in step 10 of the software and in the chat when the world opens).

  1. Start Minecraft and open the world. Its resource pack is loaded by itself — nothing to enable in the packs menu.
  2. In the chat, click [Take the wand], or type (you must be an operator / have cheats enabled):

    /function music:<name>/wand

  1. Place a block as a marker (dirt, for example), look at it and right-click with the wand. That block becomes the front left corner of the structure: it extends in front of you and to the right, and the minecarts will come towards you. The floor of the structure replaces the layer of the targeted block.
  2. While you hold the wand, the zone it takes is shown in translucent blue, with a “front of the structure” marker on the console side. Nothing is placed or broken.
    • Switch items: the zone disappears (it comes back when you take the wand again).
    • Right-click another block: the zone moves, the old one disappears.
  3. Click [Build here] in the chat, or type:

    /function music:<name>/build The chunks are loaded, then the structure is built.

  4. Click [Start the music], press the Play button of the console, or type:

    /function music:<name>/play

  1. The first note comes after the wait before the start (3 seconds by default) plus the minecarts' trip from the far end of the rails: time to get in place, for example to film. The chat says in how many seconds.
If you chose fixed coordinates in step 9 of the software, the place is already saved: go straight to step 5. The wand is only needed to change it.

To move a structure already built: /function music:<name>/remove (it is replaced with air), then a new right-click with the wand.

Several songs in the same world (full edition)

With the full edition, install as many songs as you like in the same world (step 10 of the software, one at a time): each one has its datapack, its commands and its structure, and the world's resource pack holds the sounds of all of them.

  • /function music:list: the clickable list of the songs of the world (play, stop, wand, help).
  • /function music:stop_all: stops every song.
  • Several songs can play at the same time.

The free edition keeps one song per world: installing another one takes the previous one out (it is kept in the backups).

All commands

/function music:<name>/pitch {st:-2}pitch in semitones (−24 to 24)
/function music:<name>/speed {pct:-5}speed in % (−90 to 300)
/function music:<name>/seek {s:30}go to 30 seconds
/function music:<name>/delay {s:5}wait before the minecarts leave (0 to 120 s)
/function music:<name>/laneslisten to each lane (clickable list)
/function music:<name>/status / helpcurrent settings and place / clickable help
/function music:<name>/removeremove the structure (asks for confirmation)
/function music:<name>/uninstallremove the datapack's scoreboards
/function music:list / stop_allall the songs of the world / stop everything

Players who are not operators use /trigger music.<name>.play, /trigger music.<name>.pause, /trigger music.<name>.stop, /trigger music.<name>.volume set 150 (same for tempo, pitch, speed, delay).

The button console in front of the structure does the same: play, pause, stop, volume, tempo, pitch.

A literal /play music command is impossible in vanilla Minecraft without mods: the music is controlled with datapack functions. Minecraft does not accept capitals or spaces in their names either: hence music:miss_you/play.
